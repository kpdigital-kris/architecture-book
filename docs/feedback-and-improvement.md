# Feedback and Continuous Improvement

To remain effective, Security Architecture must capture insights from:
- Delivery teams (friction points, delays)
- Cyber teams (implementation gaps)
- Business stakeholders (usability trade-offs)

This informs:
- Pattern and artefact updates
- Training needs
- Communication and engagement changes
