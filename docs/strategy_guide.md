# High-Level Guide to Creating a Strategy from Scratch

This guide provides a step-by-step approach to defining, delivering, and maintaining a technology strategy. It is intended for enterprise architects, technology leaders, and strategy practitioners.

## 1. Define the Purpose and Scope

### Why?
- Clarify the problem or opportunity.
- Determine whether the strategy is enabling or corrective.

### Scope
- Identify what's in and out of scope (technologies, teams, domains).

**Output:** Strategy charter with purpose, scope, and alignment to business goals.

---

## 2. Understand the Strategic Context

### Inputs
- Industry trends
- Internal business drivers
- Regulatory environment
- Alignment with enterprise objectives

**Output:** Strategic drivers and vision statement.

---

## 3. Conduct a Gap Assessment

### Step 1: Define the Future State
- Use a capability model or maturity framework.

### Step 2: Assess the Current State
- Gather inputs via interviews, tool reviews, and document audits.

### Step 3: Identify and Prioritise Gaps
- Create a gap matrix with Current vs Target states.

**Output:** Gap matrix and supporting visuals (heatmaps, radar charts).

---

## 4. Create the Strategy Document

### Core Sections
1. Executive Summary
2. Strategic Context & Drivers
3. Current State
4. Future State Vision
5. Strategic Pillars/Objectives
6. Gap Assessment Summary
7. Roadmap (Phases or Horizons)
8. Initiatives / Recommendations
9. Risks and Assumptions
10. KPIs and Success Metrics
11. Operating Model / Governance
12. Appendices

**Output:** Strategy document + Executive summary deck

---

## 5. Socialise and Validate

### Stakeholder Engagement
- Workshops with tech, business, and delivery teams

### Communication Strategy
- Executive briefings
- Town halls
- Internal comms (videos, articles)

**Output:** Communication plan with artefacts

---

## 6. Define the Operating and Delivery Model

### Delivery Model
- Teams, roles, ownership
- Integration with delivery frameworks

### Governance
- Steering committee, reporting cadence, issue escalation

**Output:** Operating model + implementation structure

---

## 7. Deliver the Strategy

### Execution Planning
- Convert roadmap into initiatives/epics
- Assign owners and delivery timelines

### Enablement
- Training, templates, standards
- Establish change agents and delivery champions

**Output:** Funded and governed delivery plan

---

## 8. Maintain and Evolve the Strategy

### Continuous Review
- Annual maturity reassessment
- Review based on new business and technology developments

### Feedback Loops
- Pulse surveys
- Strategy performance dashboards

**Output:** Living strategy updated regularly

---

## 9. Supporting Artefacts

| Artefact | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Capability Model | Define desired capabilities |
| Gap Assessment Matrix | Prioritise strategic investments |
| Roadmap | Visualise delivery path |
| KPI Dashboard | Track progress |
| Operating Model | Clarify ownership and delivery |
| Comms Toolkit | Support stakeholder engagement |
| Executive Deck | For senior leadership briefing |

---

## 10. Strategy Creation Checklist

| Step | Outcome |
|------|---------|
| ✅ Purpose & Scope | Strategic clarity |
| ✅ Context | Drivers and vision aligned |
| ✅ Gap Assessment | Baseline and needs identified |
| ✅ Strategy Doc | Document and deck created |
| ✅ Stakeholder Buy-in | Aligned and supported |
| ✅ Operating Model | Defined ownership |
| ✅ Roadmap | Prioritised and sequenced |
| ✅ Governance | Structure and cadence in place |
| ✅ Communication | Strategy is understood |
| ✅ Maintenance | Strategy stays relevant |
